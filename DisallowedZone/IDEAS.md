# DisallowedZone ideas

Ideas not yet planned or built. The README holds the design, the plan and the open questions; an idea moves there once it's taken on. Effort: S (an hour or two), M (about a day), L (more). "README" marks ideas the README already raises.

## First review (2026-11-22)

- **Several named zones** (M, README), each with its own switch, such as "Mech cluster" and "Insect hive".
- **Switch on by itself** (M). Turn a zone on when an infestation, mech cluster or siege appears inside it, with a message.
- **Who it applies to** (S). For each zone: everyone, colonists only, or animals only.

## Second pass

- **Exempt pawns** (S). A gizmo, saved with the pawn, that lets chosen pawns such as a squad or a firefighter ignore the zone without being drafted.
- **Stuck pawns** (S). A message naming any pawns left inside with no way out when the zone is switched on.
- **Count in the alert** (S). How many pawns are still inside each zone, so you know when it's clear.
- **Timer** (S). Switch off by itself after a chosen number of hours.
- **Paint around a thing** (S). A gizmo on a mech cluster building or a hive that paints the zone around it at a chosen radius.
