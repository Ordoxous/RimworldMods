# DisallowedZone ideas

Ideas not yet planned or built. The README holds the design, the plan and the open questions; an idea moves there once it's taken on. Ideas are grouped by area, smallest first. After each name come the effort, S (an hour or two), M (about a day) or L (more); "README" if the README already raises it; and any DLC or mod it needs. Discarded ideas stay listed with why they were dropped, so they aren't raised again.

## Ideas

### Zones

- **Who it applies to** (S). For each zone: everyone, colonists only, or animals only.
- **Paint around a thing** (S). A gizmo on a mech cluster building or a hive that paints the zone around it at a chosen radius.
- **Several named zones** (M, README). Each zone has its own name and switch, such as "Mech cluster" and "Insect hive".

### Switching on and off

- **Timer** (S). Switch off by itself after a chosen number of hours.
- **Switch on by itself** (M). Turn a zone on when an infestation, mech cluster or siege appears inside it, with a message.

### Pawns inside

- **Exempt pawns** (S). A gizmo, saved with the pawn, that lets chosen pawns such as a squad or a firefighter ignore the zone without being drafted.
- **Stuck pawns** (S). A message naming any pawns left inside with no way out when the zone is switched on.
- **Count in the alert** (S). How many pawns are still inside each zone, so you know when it's clear.

## Discarded ideas

- **MultiFloors check** (S, MultiFloors). A scenario showing pawns that MultiFloors moves onto another level to look for work skip work inside that level's zone. Turned down in the MultiFloors round (2026-11-22): the zone works per map, and the work scan reads the map the pawn is moved to, so it most likely needs no code.
