# Saddlebound ideas

Ideas not yet planned or built. The README holds the design, the plan and the open questions; an idea moves there once it's taken on. Effort: S (an hour or two), M (about a day), L (more). "README" marks ideas the README already raises.

## First review (2026-11-22)

- **The planned tests** (S, README): caravan riding counts as riding, the tracker survives save and load, and Horde Riders raids come mounted.
- **Preferred mounts in raids** (S, README). Weight Giddy-Up's mount pick toward the raiders' preferred species.
- **On-foot penalty and icons** (S, README). Mounted Glory's "fought on foot" thought, and real icons.
- **Version 2** (L, README). The Saddle Lord role and the Eating Mounts precept.

## Second pass

- **Mount funeral** (M). A ritual for a fallen mount that eases "our mount died" when it goes well, as vanilla's funeral does for a colonist.
- **Mounted traders** (S). Horde Riders trade caravans and visitors arrive mounted too, not only raiders.
- **Mount sellers** (M). A trader kind or quest from Horde Riders factions offering trained mounts of their preferred species.
- **Caravan thoughts** (S). Riding: Essential pawns are pleased by a fully mounted caravan and unhappy walking in one.
- **Taming preferred species** (S). A small bonus to taming and training the ideoligion's preferred mounts.
