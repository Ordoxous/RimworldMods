# SillyAnimalThings ideas

Ideas not yet planned or built. The README holds the design, the plan and the open questions; an idea moves there once it's taken on. Effort: S (an hour or two), M (about a day), L (more). "README" marks ideas the README already raises.

## First review (2026-11-22)

- **Planned abilities** (M each, README): Spray Musk, Leap, Fetch and Duplicate.
- **Undecided designs** (README): Nine Lives or Pounce for small cats, the alligator's Death Roll, and whether Duplicate makes babies or adults.
- **A settings page** (M). Switch each tag or ability off, and choose whether tamed animals use abilities by themselves or only when drafted.

## Second pass

- **Elephant firefighting** (M). Player elephants use Trunk Blast on fires in the home area outside combat, as a job or a think tree node.
- **Pack tactics** (M). After a Howl, the rallied pack Charges or flanks, so wolf packs feel coordinated.
- **Handler attack orders** (S). Check that tamed animals use their abilities when sent to attack by a handler, and wire it in if they don't.
- **Manhunters gnaw through doors** (M, README). Manhunting beavers chew through wooden doors and walls in their way.
- **Keep the CSVs in step** (S). A scenario that checks every built tag in the XML against `Tags.csv` and `Animals.csv`, so the design record doesn't drift.
- **Cheap tags first** (S). Leap and Quill Volley are the lowest effort and would add two tags quickly.
