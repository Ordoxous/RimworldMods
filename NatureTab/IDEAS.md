# NatureTab ideas

Ideas not yet planned or built. The README holds the design, the plan and the open questions; an idea moves there once it's taken on. Effort: S (an hour or two), M (about a day), L (more). "README" marks ideas the README already raises.

## First review (2026-11-22)

- **Hotkeys for Plants and Minerals** (S, README). Unbound key bindings, like F4 and F5 for Animals and Wildlife.
- **Fit narrow screens** (S, README). "Within: Any distance" is cut off on its 130 px button, and the 1026 px window clips its right-hand buttons below 1280 px.
- **Group rows by product** (M, README). A toggle that merges kinds giving the same thing, such as several modded berry bushes, into one row.
- **A "Ready on" column** (S). The date the next plants are ready, easier to read across seasons than days until ready.
- **More mineral actions** (M). Smooth or Deconstruct for stone, a warning for roof collapse once overhead cells are marked, and veins ordered by distance from a stockpile.

## Second pass

- **Deep resources** (M). Rows on Minerals for what the ground-penetrating scanner has found (`deepResourceGrid`), with counts and a jump to each patch, to help place deep drills.
- **Mining time** (S). A column for the work to mine the row's cells at a typical miner's speed, next to Yield and Value.
- **Harvest at risk only** (S). A row button that marks only the plants dying, ageing out or about to freeze.
- **Keep harvested** (M). A per-kind checkbox, saved with the game, that marks that kind's ready plants once a day within the page's area and distance. Wild healroot and berries get picked without opening the tab.
- **Ready alert** (S). An optional alert when a kind the player picked, such as healroot or ambrosia, has plants ready.
- **Nutrition** (S). A column with the total nutrition of what's ready, for food plants: can wild berries carry the colony through winter?
- **Wildlife by species** (M). A summary over the Wildlife page, one row per species, with counts and Hunt all and Tame buttons, in the style of Plants and Minerals.
- **Fish** (L, Odyssey). Fish population per body of water and fishing zone, if Odyssey's fishing data can be read cheaply.
- **Product in stock** (S). Right-clicking a product's icon jumps to the nearest stockpile holding it, to compare stock with what's growing.
