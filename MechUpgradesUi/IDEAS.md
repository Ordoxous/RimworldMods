# MechUpgradesUi ideas

Ideas not yet planned or built. The README holds the design, the plan and the open questions; an idea moves there once it's taken on. Ideas are grouped by area, smallest first. After each name come the effort, S (an hour or two), M (about a day) or L (more); "README" if the README already raises it; and any DLC or mod it needs. Discarded ideas stay listed with why they were dropped, so they aren't raised again.

## Ideas

### Policies

- **Default policy for each mech type** (S). Lifters get one policy, militors another.
- **Policy from a mech** (S). A menu entry that makes a policy from a mech's installed upgrades.
- **Control groups** (S). Assign a policy to every mech in a mechanitor's control group at once.
- **Points in the editor** (S). The preview shows points used and left for the chosen mech type as the policy changes.

### Stock and upgraders

- **Unreachable upgrader alert** (S). Mechs with free points and matching stock, but no upgrader they can reach or use.
- **Shared stock** (M, README). Reserve stock across mechs, so two mechs don't both expect the same item.
- **Keep in stock** (M). Queue assembler bills for upgrades the policies want but nothing has in stock.

### Upkeep

- **Showcase the ranking** (S, README). A clip dragging an upgrade up the ranked list.
