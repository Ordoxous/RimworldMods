# ColonistBarJobs ideas

Ideas not yet planned or built. The README holds the design, the plan and the open questions; an idea moves there once it's taken on. Effort: S (an hour or two), M (about a day), L (more). "README" marks ideas the README already raises.

## First review (2026-11-22)

- **More mappings** (S, README). Warden, Cleaning, Firefighter and Complex Jobs' work types fall back to an odd icon or none.
- **Mechs** (M, README). Icons in the mechanitor's mech group, since mechs aren't on the vanilla bar.
- **A fuller tooltip** (S). The job's progress, and the bill's name for crafting.

## Second pass

- **The product, not the bench** (S). An option to show what a bill makes, such as the meal or the rifle, instead of the workbench.
- **Progress ring** (M). A thin ring round the icon that fills with the job's progress, for jobs that have one: bills, construction, research.
- **Click to jump** (S). Clicking the icon jumps the camera to the job's target.
- **Queued jobs** (S). The tooltip lists the colonist's queued jobs after the current one.
- **Long idle** (S). A distinct icon for a colonist who has been idle for over an hour, so they stand out.
