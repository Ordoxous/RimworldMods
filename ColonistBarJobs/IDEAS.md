# ColonistBarJobs ideas

Ideas not yet planned or built. The README holds the design, the plan and the open questions; an idea moves there once it's taken on. Ideas are grouped by area, smallest first. After each name come the effort, S (an hour or two), M (about a day) or L (more); "README" if the README already raises it; and any DLC or mod it needs. Discarded ideas stay listed with why they were dropped, so they aren't raised again.

## Ideas

None yet. More mappings, mechs (in the control group gizmos), the fuller tooltip and click to jump were built on 2026-11-22 and are in the README.

## Discarded ideas

Dropped by the developer on 2026-11-22, when the rest were built:

- **The product, not the bench** (S). An option to show what a bill makes, such as the meal or the rifle, instead of the workbench. The tooltip now names the bill instead.
- **Long idle** (S). A distinct icon for a colonist who has been idle for over an hour, so they stand out.
- **Progress ring** (M). A thin ring round the icon that fills with the job's progress. The tooltip now gives the progress instead.
- **Queued jobs** (S). The tooltip lists the colonist's queued jobs after the current one.
