# LegendaryIsUnique ideas

Ideas not yet planned or built. The README holds the design, the plan and the open questions; an idea moves there once it's taken on. Effort: S (an hour or two), M (about a day), L (more). "README" marks ideas the README already raises.

## First review (2026-11-22)

- **Choose the variant** (M). Pick from the unique traits, or reroll once at a cost, instead of a random unique.
- **Allow list** (S). Choose in the settings which weapons can turn unique.
- **Say so** (S). A "became unique" line in the craft letter or message.

## Second pass

- **A chance for each quality** (S). Such as Excellent 5%, Masterwork 20%, Legendary 100%, instead of one threshold and one chance.
- **Inspired creativity** (S). An inspired craft always turns unique, or has a higher chance.
- **More traits with skill** (M). A more skilled crafter rolls more or better unique traits.
- **Other mods' uniques** (M). Map any mod's unique variants through a def extension or a settings list, not only the `_Unique` naming rule.
