# TabGroups ideas

Ideas not yet planned or built. The README holds the design, the plan and the open questions; an idea moves there once it's taken on. Ideas are grouped by area, smallest first. After each name come the effort, S (an hour or two), M (about a day) or L (more); "README" if the README already raises it; and any DLC or mod it needs. Discarded ideas stay listed with why they were dropped, so they aren't raised again.

## Ideas

### Groups

- **Share a layout** (S). Copy and paste the saved assignments as a string, to move a layout between profiles or players.
- **Custom groups** (M). Add, rename, reorder and delete groups in the settings, not only move tabs between the five fixed ones.
- **Group icons** (M, README). Icons on the group buttons, so they match mods like Bradson's Main Button Icons.
- **Where new buttons go** (M). Buttons from mods added later go on the bar; an option could send unknown buttons to Other or Tools instead.

### The group menu

- **Open on hover** (S). An option to show the member list on hover, and a hotkey for each group.
- **Move from the menu** (S). Shift-click a member in a group's list to put it back on the bar or move it to another group, without opening the settings.
- **Middle-click opens the last member used** (S). The old quick access as an extra, without the button changing under you.
- **Search long groups** (S). A filter box when a group has more than about ten members.
